# Observation-subject concept sources

`observation_subject` is a source-text field, not a Wikidata identifier. The following are **candidate senses for nonblank botanical text**, not assignments to individual observations. In particular, a blank `observation_subject` is not evidence for “leaf.” Keep the original field text beside any separately curated concept URI; retain the source record and the mapping's reviewer/method and date so that a changed interpretation can be audited. RDF distinguishes literals from IRIs; PROV-O defines derivation from a source entity, attribution to an agent and generation by an activity ([RDF 1.1 Concepts](https://www.w3.org/TR/rdf11-concepts/#section-Graph-Literal), [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/#starting-points), [PROV vocabulary](https://www.w3.org/ns/prov-o)).

**Identity and hierarchy checks.** Wikidata's [`wbsearchentities` API](https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php?action=help&modules=wbsearchentities) searches labels/aliases by language and returns matched terms plus displayed descriptions; a [live `leaf` search](https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php?action=wbsearchentities&search=leaf&language=en&type=item&limit=5&format=json) also returns a surname and a book leaf. Search order or spelling alone cannot establish the botanical sense; `Stems`, `bud` and `bark` likewise need context (see [search for Stems](https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php?action=wbsearchentities&search=stems&language=en&type=item&limit=5&format=json), [bud](https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php?action=wbsearchentities&search=bud&language=en&type=item&limit=5&format=json), [bark](https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php?action=wbsearchentities&search=bark&language=en&type=item&limit=5&format=json)). Read the candidate's English label, description, aliases and claims in [EntityData JSON](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access#Linked_Data_Interface_(URI)) or [`wbgetentities`](https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php?action=help&modules=wbgetentities), and distinguish a particular entity from its names: the concept URI is `http://www.wikidata.org/entity/Q33971`, whereas `https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:EntityData/Q33971.json` is its data document ([Wikidata data access](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access#Linked_Data_Interface_(URI))).

At the checked [Q33971 EntityData revision 2568841875](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:EntityData/Q33971.json?revision=2568841875), `subclass of (P279)` points to [plant organ (Q24060707)](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:EntityData/Q24060707.json), while `instance of (P31)` points to [plant structure (Q20011319)](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:EntityData/Q20011319.json). These are **different relations**: P279 relates classes and is transitive; P31 relates an instance to a class and is not transitive; `part of (P361)` expresses composition, not a subtype. Neither a leaf nor a flower becomes an instance/subclass of the organism's taxon merely because it occurs on that organism ([Wikidata membership-property guide](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Basic_membership_properties)); taxonomic parentage instead has the distinct [parent taxon (P171)](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P171). Thus an anatomical observation target and the observed organism/taxon must remain distinguishable claims.

For relationship questions after a candidate is known, [Wikidata Query Service](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access#Wikidata_Query_Service) exposes SPARQL at `https://query.wikidata.org/sparql`; it explicitly advises **search**, not SPARQL `FILTER(REGEX(...))`, for fuzzy text discovery. The independently operated [QLever Wikidata UI](https://qlever.dev/wikidata) also exposes a Wikidata SPARQL interface; its availability, refresh lag and endpoint contract should not be assumed identical to Wikidata's own services. Both query results and the live entity pages may change; the cited Q33971 revision fixes only the example leaf's claims, not the other candidates or any source-row interpretation ([EntityData revisions](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access#Revisions_and_caching)).
